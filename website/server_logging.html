Server Logging

Introduction

There's always been this narrative that companies and websites are stealing our data. It's gotten so prevalent that we now have laws requiring us to accept or decline cookies—which seemed like some sort of magic to me before I actually understood how websites work.

As I've gotten deeper into computer science, web development, and data engineering, I've come to understand what information you're actually giving away when you visit a website. The purpose of this page is to break down what data you're sharing regardless of which cookies you accept, why websites need this data, and what could go wrong if it falls into the wrong hands.
